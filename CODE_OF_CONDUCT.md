# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in Desk to be free of harassment for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful and constructive in issues and reviews
- Accepting criticism about the code
- Focusing on what is best for the people who use Desk

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Publishing someone else's private information, including contact details or credentials, without permission
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Project maintainers will remove comments, issues, and commits that violate this code, and may ban contributors for severe or repeated behavior.

Report conduct concerns through a GitHub issue if it is safe to do so in public, or through [private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/LotusmirkAerospace/lotusmirk-desk/security/advisories/new) if the report itself contains private information.
